        SMALL TASK / REAL PHYSICS
        
Reach. Close. Lift.
Carry. Let go.

        

          TIAGo sorts blue and red boxes into matching bins, then returns for the next one. The fingers hold each box through simulated contact and
          friction.
        

        

          Analytic grasp planning, Cartesian inverse kinematics and a differential-drive controller supply the motion. The controller reads known
          object poses directly: there is no camera perception, learning or backend.
        

        
Objects are recycled after settling in a bin. Failed grasps trigger retreat and retries, so the shift can keep going.

        

          Adapted from MuJoCo Menagerie's TIAGo. Simplified collision geometry, fixed torso and head, passive caster approximation. Orbit with a mouse
          or one finger; zoom with a wheel or pinch.
