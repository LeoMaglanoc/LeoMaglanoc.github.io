A goal. A grasp.
A thousand tiny adjustments.

The hand observes its joints, the cube, and your desired orientation, then issues new finger targets 20 times per simulated second. Every movement comes from contact physics and a goal-conditioned reinforcement learning policy.

The original PPO behavior was trained by Wuji Technology. This demo ports their released Hand 1 actor and matching model to the browser, with interactive goals, live diagnostics, and touch controls. No distillation or retraining was needed.
