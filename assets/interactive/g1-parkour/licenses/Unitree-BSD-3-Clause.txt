Unitree Robotics - robot descriptions and SDK joint maps
Original Source: https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros
========================================================================
Applies to the Unitree G1 and H1 robot descriptions redistributed in this
repository (URDF, MJCF, and the STL/DAE mesh trees under
src/holosoma/holosoma/data/robots/g1/ and .../h1/, and derivatives of them such
as g1_29dof_halfspherehand.xml and the combined_* collision meshes), and to the
Unitree joint-index maps inlined in
src/holosoma/holosoma/sdk/unitree_high_level/arm_client/const.py.
