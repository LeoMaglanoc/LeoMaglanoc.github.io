A NOTE ON THE MODEL

Simple by design.
Curiosity encouraged.

This is a simplified wind-driven model, not a measurement of air quality. It tracks original indoor air as a conservative tracer through a 12 × 6 m apartment. Single-window exchange is an illustrative approximation. Temperature, buoyancy, outdoor pollution and CO₂ are not modeled; zero wind means no outdoor exchange. Explore the assumptions ↗
