IMITATION ≠ EXECUTION

Action fit is only part of the story.

Normalized action MSE on held-out demonstrations. A lower imitation loss does not guarantee a successful closed-loop rollout: small errors compound, and the robot sees states absent from the demonstrations.

Selections change prerecorded artifacts. Both policies replay at the same simulation clock; shorter episodes hold their final frame. The result counts every scheduled rollout, including failures.

Data, model & evaluation
What this experiment cannot tell us

Three short human videos from one participant are a feasibility subset. Detector boxes, contact estimates and wrist matching are noisy. Camera motion is included in image-space displacement. The two robot scenes also differ, so this does not isolate language grounding.

Five held-out starts per task and three training seeds provide an exploratory comparison, not a large benchmark. No real robot, large VLA training, or RL improvement is claimed.

Reproduce & continue training

Scripts, fixed configuration, splits, training logs and restoration instructions live with the source. Local checkpoints include model and optimizer state, normalization, epoch, RNG and data-loader state. Both validation-selected and last-epoch checkpoints are retained.
