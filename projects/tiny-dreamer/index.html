How does this work? +

This is a small Dreamer-inspired implementation for understanding latent world models and imagination-based reinforcement learning.

  1. The agent collects observations and rewards from real MuJoCo physics.
  2. A recurrent state-space model (RSSM) learns a compressed, predictive belief about that world.
  3. The actor practices on imagined trajectories generated by the learned model. Its critic estimates future return.
  4. Here, the solid pole shows what physics does. Translucent blue poles show decoded neural predictions at several future times.
  5. Push the cart to disturb the prediction. New observations update the belief, and a fresh dream follows.

The deployed policy runs locally on your device. Training used vector observations and CPU PyTorch; this is not a reproduction of full DreamerV3. Reward predictions can have small errors outside the real reward’s 0–1 range. The error readout compares a one-step neural prediction with the following physical observation.
