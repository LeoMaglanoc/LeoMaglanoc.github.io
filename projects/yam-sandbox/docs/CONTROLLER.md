# Native controller and changes from the ZIP (checkpoint B)

The original YAM link frames, joint limits, per-link inertias, arm actuator gains, paired-finger equality, all visual STL files and all collision primitives are preserved. The original model uses primitive collision shapes alongside visual meshes; it does not use detailed STL triangle collision for every link.

Changes: gripper kp=600 N/m, kv=20 N·s/m, bounded force ±12 N (original kp100/kv10); cylinder half-height .045 m instead of .031 m, same .024 radius/.09 kg; cylinder contact condim=6, friction 1.1/.02/.002 for sliding/torsion/rolling (original condim3, friction1.1/.1/.03 whose extra components were inactive); distinct goals left(-.22,-.22), right(.22,-.18) instead of one right target. No robot link, collision, gravity, attachment or pose-replay shortcuts.

Experiments: original grip lifted the cube but slipped during transfer; original cylinder geometry slipped/rolled from the pads even after force tuning. Cartesian interpolation rather than single joint-space transfers improved cube retention. A .09 m tall cylinder (instead of .062 m) puts the finger pad contact nearer its center of mass and reliably retains it. The original tabletop and object masses are unchanged. Reachable grasp_site Z=.749, lift/transfer=.85, release=.753, retreat=.88 m. Approach .88 rather than unreachable1.03. The original contact geometry demands care near the tabletop; trajectory sweeps reject table penetration greater than1 mm.

Offline bounded least-squares IK uses exact MuJoCo FK and rotation residuals to generate a 25-sample Cartesian path per phase, with joint-limit margins and cube-symmetric yaw candidates. The runtime receives actuator targets only. Runtime physics/control steps at500 Hz with the original2 ms timestep. Smooth cubic target interpolation, actual joint error/velocity gating, contact verification and phase timeouts close the loop. No learned policy. Fixed target positions, not general object discovery or replanning.

Grasp oracle: at least one finger-object contact AND measured lift≥.05 m above the post-reset settled center continuously for.2 simulated seconds. Release/place oracle: tabletop contact, no finger contact, horizontal error<.05 m, linear speed<.05 m/s, center height within.015 m of initial settled height for.5 s. Lost contact for>.15 s after verified lift fails transport (including lowering until table contact). Completed timers alone never indicate success.

Evaluation: predeclared20 nominal resets+10 offsets (±5 mm grid, see evaluate.py) per object; negative controls disable object contacts and must fail. `results/native-evaluation.json` includes individual phase/contact/pose/error traces and reasons. Runtime traces use qpos/qvel after mj_step (renderer uses mjData transforms). Independent review caught the lost-grasp height-gate bug; it was fixed and the full batch rerun. Unit tests exercise late contact removal, FK, limits, gripper direction, and no live pose writes.

Checkpoints preserve the entire mjSTATE_INTEGRATION vector plus controller state, scene/reference/controller SHA256 and engine version. Resume requires exact matching inputs. `tools/save_checkpoints.py checkpoints/cube-lifted.json` resumes and evaluates; see checkpoint resume results. These are physics/controller snapshots; there is no training or neural-network state.
