Leonardo Maglanoc

I’m Leo, a robot learning and applied AI engineer with an M.Sc. in Robotics, Cognition, and Intelligence from TUM. I’ve worked across the robotics and AI stack, with projects spanning language-guided dexterous manipulation for my master’s thesis at a robotics startup, neuromorphic computing at BMW Research, and safe human-robot collaboration research at TUM published in IEEE T-RO.

Today, I’m focused on foundation models for physical AI and exploring agentic AI through hackathons. Born in Norway and raised in Germany, I’m particularly excited to contribute to Europe’s deep-tech ecosystem and build ambitious robotics and AI systems here.

Featured Projects

SO-101 Robot Learning and Foundation Models

End-to-end visuomotor policy learning on a physical SO-101 manipulator. Built the full imitation-learning pipeline with LeRobot: teleoperation, dataset collection, ACT and SmolVLA training, and real-robot policy deployment.

MSc Thesis at a Robotics Startup

Language-guided tool-motion generation for dexterous manipulation. Grounds natural-language instructions in parameterized SE(3) motion primitives, combining LLM-level reasoning with learned low-level policies.

startup

Neuromorphic Computing at BMW Research

Neuro-inspired computing with Oscillatory Neural Networks in collaboration with IBM. Built Python/PyTorch simulators and collected tactile interaction data from 10 participants for a human-machine interaction demonstrator.

EU project

Safe Human-Robot Collaboration at TUM

Runtime safety for physical human-robot interaction using reachability analysis and verification. Contributed to SaRA-Shield, providing formal safety guarantees for autonomous manipulation; published in IEEE Transactions on Robotics.
